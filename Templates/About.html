Company Overview

TechNova Solutions is a full-stack software development company founded in 2020, headquartered in Bengaluru, India. We specialize in delivering high-performance technology solutions including custom web applications, mobile apps, enterprise software, and scalable cloud platforms. With a passion for innovation, we’ve served 70+ global clients and delivered 150+ successful projects across industries such as fintech, healthcare, education, and logistics.

Our talented engineers and designers collaborate with clients to craft exceptional digital experiences and long-term value. Whether it’s launching a startup or modernizing a legacy system, TechNova is your partner in technology-driven growth.
